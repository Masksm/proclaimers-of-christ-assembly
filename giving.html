YOUR GENEROSITY MATTERS

Partner in the work.

Giving is a voluntary expression of worship and a practical way to support the work of the church and its service to people.

We want every contribution to be made through a verified channel and with clear information.

Official bank details and payment channels are not published here because they have not yet been verified for public display. Do not send money to an account based on an unverified message or social media post.

A CLEAR AND SAFE PROCESS

Before you give.

Use these simple checks until the church publishes its official giving instructions.

01

Confirm the channel

Contact the church through an official communication route to request current giving instructions.

02

Verify the details

Confirm the account name, institution and payment purpose before making a transfer.

03

Keep your record

Retain your transaction receipt and contact the church if you need acknowledgement or clarification.

No payment form or bank account details are displayed until church leadership supplies and verifies them.
